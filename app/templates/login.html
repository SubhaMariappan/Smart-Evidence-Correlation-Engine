⚡ How SECE Operates (3-Stage Forensic Processing Pipeline)

1. Cryptographic Ingestion & Baseline Vault:

Heterogeneous evidence files (.txt, .csv, .pdf) are streamed through 64KB chunk SHA-256 hashing upon entry. Baseline digests are permanently indexed into the forensic database to guarantee non-repudiation and detect disk-level tampering during on-demand re-verification audits.

2. Modular Parsing & Indicator Extraction:

Dedicated factory parsers extract text content and technical file structure into structured JSONB. Deterministic regex engines locate high-confidence forensic indicators (EMAIL, IPV4, PHONE_NUMBER), while NLP Named Entity Recognition (NER) detects PERSON and LOCATION entities, capturing exact start/end character offsets for verifiable provenance.

3. Candidate Correlation & Graph Discovery:

The correlation engine inspects co-occurrences and lexical similarity across separate evidence files within the active case. Hypotheses are scored and staged as candidate matches for investigator adjudication (Accept or Reject), while confirmed links feed real-time interactive node-link relationship graphs.

📜 Forensic Terms & Conditions / Data Privacy Agreement

  • Mandatory Audit Logging: All evidence ingestion, parser execution, entity extraction, correlation pipelines, and status modifications are recorded as permanent, append-only chain-of-custody audit logs with investigator UUID and UTC timestamps.
  • Decision-Support Boundary: SECE is strictly a forensic decision-support workstation. Algorithmic inferences and match confidence scores are assistive hypotheses and require formal human verification by an authorized investigator or administrator before being admitted into forensic case files.
  • Access Control & Isolation: Case containers and ingested vault evidence are segregated. Access is governed under strict Two-Role RBAC: ADMIN for case governance and oversight, and INVESTIGATOR for evidence processing and candidate adjudication.
